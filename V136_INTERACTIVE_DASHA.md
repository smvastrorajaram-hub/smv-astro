# V136 Interactive Vimshottari Dasha

Adds touch/click drill-down: Mahadasha -> Antardasha (Bhukti) -> Pratyantardasha.

The Vimshottari sequence is Ketu, Venus, Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, with 120 total years. Each Mahadasha is divided into 9 Antardashas and each Antardasha into 9 Pratyantardashas proportionally. The first Mahadasha is anchored to the Moon nakshatra and birth balance; subperiods are generated from the full parent period and clipped at birth for the first period.

UI is mobile-friendly and expands levels on touch.
